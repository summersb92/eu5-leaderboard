      How it works
      
    
      A location's max control comes mostly from its proximity: 0.75% per point.
      Proximity starts at 100 in the capital and 80 at a local governor, and every step to a neighbouring
      location costs some — less along roads and rivers, more through hills, forests and mountains, cheap
      at sea where you have maritime presence. This page rebuilds that spread for your nation from the save
      and the game's map, then tries every location as a new governor or capital.
      
    
      
      
      What does “settled tax base” mean?
        
      Each location's possible tax (its tax at full control, from the save) times the max control its
          proximity gives. Control drifts towards that maximum over years, so gains are what you'd see once it has
          settled. Integration, satisfaction, armies and buildings also move max control; they're left out, so
          treat gains as a way to compare places, not a promise.
      
      
      How accurate is it?
        
      The model re-derives your current proximity from your capital and governors and compares it to the
          value the save records for every location; the result shows how close it got. Your nation's own proximity
          bonuses (advances, laws, societal values) are folded into one factor fitted to your save. Routes over
          lagoons and short sea crossings are the least accurate part.
      
      
      Where can a local governor go?
        
      In a city with a road to the capital, not in the capital or a subject's land, and only as many as your
          advances allow (one from the Age of Discovery). Untick Only where one can be built now to see
          where one would pay off once you've upgraded a town or laid a road.
      
      
      Does moving the capital keep my governors?
        
      The capital rankings keep your existing governors where they are. A governor that loses its road to
          the new capital is removed by the game, so check the roads before moving.
