How it works

    
The Borough Privileges advance (Age of Discovery) unlocks nine production rights. Each adds
      +20% (Tooling, Naval Supplies and Masonry: +25–30%) output of two or three goods in the location and costs 5%
      production efficiency there. A town holds one urban right, a city two, a megalopolis three. This page reads what
      each location makes from your save — its RGO workers and every building's level, staffing and production method
      — scales it to the supply its market records, and prices it at that market's prices.

    

      
Why does the RGO get the bonus but not the penalty?
        
A right's goods bonus (e.g. local_dyes_output_modifier) changes all output of that good in the
          location, RGO included — Naval Supplies Rights boosting tar, a raw material, shows it. The penalty is
          local_production_efficiency, which the game applies to buildings; RGOs use a separate raw material
          output modifier. Tick −5% efficiency hits the RGO too for the pessimistic case.

      
Danzig: Printing or Textile Rights?
        
Both boost dyes, so the dyes RGO gains the same either way. The difference is the buildings: Printing boosts paper
          and books (a scriptorium, a paper guild), Textile boosts cloth and fine cloth. Whichever set of guilds is bigger in
          the city wins, and the penalty is the same 5% of every building's output in both cases.

      
What does “wool from Nowe” mean?
        
A building gets a production efficiency bonus when the raw materials it uses are made by an RGO in the same
          province. Each boosted building names the inputs of the production method it's running that an RGO in the
          province makes, and where (“its own RGO” for the location itself). Those guilds are the ones a right
          pays best on, and they're worth growing; the bonus itself is already in the output the save records.

      
What about the rights it already has?
        
Rights already granted count against the slots. When a town or city is full the table still shows what the best
          production right would be worth, so you can weigh it against what's there (Lübeck rights' marketplace levels, a
          staple port…) — this page doesn't value those.

      
How accurate is it?
        
Output follows the save: each building's output per level times its level and staffing, and the RGO's workers,
          scaled so the location's share of its market matches the market's recorded supply. The page assumes a right's
          bonus and penalty add up (+20% − 5% = +15% for a boosted good). Prices move as supply grows, so a big boost to
          a good your market already has plenty of will be worth a little less than shown.
