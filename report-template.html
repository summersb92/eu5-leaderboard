The map

▸

Every location tinted by owner, in the same colors EU V's own map mode uses for each nation — everyone else's territory sits in flat grey for context. Dependencies (vassals, fiefdoms, tributaries, and the like) are hatched in their overlord's color; a personal union's junior partner is hatched with its own secondary color instead of white, so it reads apart from an ordinary subject.

The whole world, at the save’s date: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move, and hover (or tap) any location for its owner, population, development, control and tax. The map modes colour every location by one figure.
