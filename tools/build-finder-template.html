Treasury per level = profit × wealth factor (1.06 buildings, 0.76 RGOs) × control × (the location's estate-weighted tax rate − extra spending) − upkeep. The new tax base is profit × wealth factor × control; court, diplomatic and stability spending each grow by 10% of it × their slider (× the nation's cost modifiers, read from the save), shown as "Spending/lv". "Min profit" is the monthly profit per level the building needs to cover its financing. "Pays up to" is the highest yearly interest rate at which it still covers the interest. New buildings use the average profit per level of that building elsewhere in the same market. Tinted rows are in the plan.
